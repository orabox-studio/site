SpareRemote Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

SpareRemote (“we”, “the app”) is a Wi‑Fi TV remote control application for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Summary

  • TV control happens on your local Wi‑Fi network.
  • We do not require an account to use the remote.
  • Device names, IPs, and pairing tokens stay on your phone for reconnection.
  • Crash reports are sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics; feature flags are loaded from Firebase Remote Config. Ads are off.

Information we process on your device

  • Local network data: discovered TV IP addresses, brand/model hints, MAC addresses when available, and connection status. Used only to find and control your TVs.
  • Pairing tokens / credentials: stored securely on your device so you can reconnect without pairing every time.
  • App preferences: such as last connected TV and quick-access app shortcuts.

Internet permissions

The app uses network access to talk to TVs on your LAN and, when enabled, to load remote configuration, analytics, crash reporting, or ads from Google services.

Analytics, crash reporting, and ads

SpareRemote uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Analytics where enabled) and may later use and Google AdMob. These services can collect device and usage information according to Google’s policies when enabled. Ads are off by default and can be turned on remotely later.

Advertising ID may be used if ads are enabled. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If Google services are enabled, data may be processed by Google as a service provider under their terms.

Children

SpareRemote is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove local data stored by SpareRemote.
  • Revoke network permissions in Android settings to stop LAN discovery/control.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.
