SpareRemote Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

SpareRemote (“we”, “the app”) is a Wi‑Fi TV remote control application for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Android and iOS do not use the same Google services.

Summary

  • TV control happens on your local Wi‑Fi network.
  • We do not require an account to use the remote.
  • Device names, IPs, and pairing tokens stay on your phone for reconnection.
  • On iOS, the current App Store build does not initialize Firebase or AdMob.
  • On Android, crash reports can go to Google Firebase Crashlytics and feature flags can load from Firebase Remote Config. Ads are off by default.

Information we process on your device

  • Local network data: discovered TV IP addresses, brand/model hints, MAC addresses when available, and connection status. Used only to find and control your TVs.
  • Pairing tokens / credentials: stored securely on your device so you can reconnect without pairing every time.
  • App preferences: such as last connected TV and quick-access app shortcuts.

Internet permissions

The app uses network access to talk to TVs on your LAN. On Android, when those features are enabled, it may also load remote configuration, analytics, crash reporting, or ads from Google services.

Android: analytics, crash reporting, and ads

The Android app can use Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Analytics where enabled) and Google AdMob. These services can collect device and usage information according to Google’s policies when enabled. Ads are off by default and can be turned on remotely later.

Advertising ID may be used on Android if ads are enabled. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.

iOS: no Firebase or AdMob in the current App Store build

In the current iOS App Store build, SpareRemote does not initialize Firebase (Crashlytics, Remote Config, or Analytics) and does not initialize AdMob or request App Tracking Transparency. TV discovery and control stay on the local network. Saved device names, IPs, pairing tokens, and preferences stay on the device. The iOS app can open this policy in a browser and can show Apple’s in-app review prompt.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If Google services are enabled, data may be processed by Google as a service provider under their terms.

Children

SpareRemote is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove local data stored by SpareRemote.
  • Revoke local-network / Wi‑Fi permissions in system settings to stop LAN discovery/control.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.
