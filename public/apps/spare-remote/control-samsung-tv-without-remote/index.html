How to control a Samsung TV without the remote

Remote lost in the sofa, broken, or the TV came without one? Here are 5 ways to keep using your Samsung TV, from free options built into the TV to a proper replacement.

1. Use the control button on the TV

Every Samsung TV has a control button or small joystick. On most recent models it sits under the middle of the bottom edge, below the Samsung logo; on older ones it is on the back, bottom right when you face the screen.

  • Short press: turn on, or open the control menu when the TV is on.
  • Press again: move between Power, Volume, Channel and Source.
  • Press and hold: select the highlighted item.

This is enough to switch the TV on, change the volume or pick an HDMI input, and to select Allow when a phone asks to connect.

2. Plug in a USB keyboard (or mouse)

Samsung TVs accept a standard USB keyboard. Plug it into a USB port on the TV and use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc to move through menus and type passwords. It is the most reliable way to accept a connection prompt when the TV button only opens its own menu.

3. Use Samsung SmartThings on your phone

Samsung's official SmartThings app (Android and iPhone) has a built‑in remote. Put the phone on the same Wi‑Fi as the TV, sign in with a Samsung account, then add the TV under Devices. Depending on the model year you confirm with a PIN shown on the TV (2017–2019) or by selecting OK on the TV (2020 and later, use method 1 or 2). On 2025 models from the U8000F up, the TV button can show a Mobile Remote QR code to scan.

If you prefer not to use a Samsung account, a Wi‑Fi remote app works too. 5. Get the right replacement remote

Find the model code on the sticker on the back of the TV (for example UE55…, QE65…, UN65…). Search it on Samsung's parts site or a retailer to get the matching remote, or buy a universal remote that lists Samsung support. Remotes from older Samsung TVs often handle power, volume, channels and menus too.

Questions

Where is the button on a Samsung TV?

On most models it is a small control stick or button under the middle of the bottom edge, below the Samsung logo. On some it is on the back, bottom right when you face the screen. Short press opens the control menu, press again to move, press and hold to select.

How do I press Allow on a Samsung TV without the remote?

When the Allow prompt appears, press the TV control button until Allow is highlighted, then hold it for about two seconds. If that does not work, plug a USB keyboard into the TV, use the arrow keys to highlight Allow and press Enter.

Can I use my phone as a remote without an IR blaster?

Yes. Samsung SmartThings and Wi‑Fi remote apps such as SpareRemote control the TV over your home network, so the phone only needs to be on the same Wi‑Fi as the TV.

Can I turn on a Samsung TV with my phone?

Often yes. Enable Power On with Mobile in the TV's network expert settings (the name varies by year). The TV can then be woken from standby over Wi‑Fi. It will not work if the TV was unplugged.

Which replacement remote do I need?

Check the model code on the sticker on the back of the TV and search for it on Samsung's parts site or a retailer. Most Samsung‑compatible universal remotes also cover the basics.
