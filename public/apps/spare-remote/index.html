Questions

Can I use my phone as a Samsung TV remote?

Yes. Install SpareRemote on your Android phone, connect the phone to the same Wi‑Fi as your Samsung Smart TV, open the app and tap your TV. The first time, choose Allow on the TV screen. You then get a d‑pad, volume, channels, a number pad, color keys, a touchpad and 100+ extra Samsung keys.

Does my phone need an IR blaster?

No. SpareRemote talks to the TV over your home Wi‑Fi network, so it works on phones without an infrared (IR) emitter.

Do the phone and the TV have to be on the same Wi‑Fi?

Yes. Both must be on the same local network. If the TV is not found automatically, you can type its IP address (Settings → General → Network → Network Status on most Samsung TVs).

I lost my Samsung TV remote. What can I do?

Turn the TV on with the power button on the TV itself, connect it to your Wi‑Fi once, then use SpareRemote on your phone as a replacement remote while you order a new one — or keep it as a permanent spare.

Can SpareRemote turn my Samsung TV on?

It can wake the TV over Wi‑Fi (Wake‑on‑LAN) on TVs that support it and keep their network active in standby. Some models fully power off the network and must be turned on with the TV button the first time.

Which other TVs work?

Besides Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen), SpareRemote works with LG (webOS), Roku, Sony Bravia, Android TV / Google TV and Fire TV.
